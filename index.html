Answers to the questions we hear most

What is the difference between Free, Core, and Strategic?

Free follows one company with monthly checks and stays free for life with no card needed. Core checks weekly and adds insights, battlecards, and suggested actions for $2,000 per month. Strategic combines daily checks with research by our team across 20+ categories for you and each competitor, for $5,000 per month.

Who is Free for?

Free is for you if you want to follow one competitor with monthly checks, a basic report, and a battlecard. Your account stays free for life with no card needed.

When should I move up to Core?

Move up to Core when you need weekly checks across up to 10 companies, with insights on important moves, battlecards, and suggested actions. Core costs $2,000 per month and includes a weekly report and a monthly summary.

What does Strategic add beyond Core?

Strategic adds daily checks and research by our team across 20+ categories for your company and each competitor. You receive a comprehensive report with prioritized recommendations and a roadmap, the Compdawg Top Dawg Matrix, and a monthly summary reviewed by our team. Strategic costs $5,000 per month.

Can I start on Free and upgrade later?

Yes, start with one company on Free and upgrade when you need weekly insights on Core or daily checks and research by our team on Strategic. Free stays free for life with no card needed.

What is the free competitor report?

Your free for life account shows how to compete against the competitor you add, with a basic report and a battlecard

Is the free account really free for life?

Yes, the free plan stays free for life with no card needed, and you can move up to more timely checks and insights whenever you want

How often do you check?

We check every source on your plan's schedule: monthly on Free, weekly on Core, and daily on Strategic

What do you watch?

For each competitor we watch news, posts, hiring, filings, ads, executive moves, pricing, positioning, launches, reviews, sentiment, and search visibility

What do I do with what you find?

Every important move comes with a summary on how to act on it, so leadership can see the opening and respond

Do I need to set anything up?

Add your competitors and we do the rest

Who gets the summaries?

Summaries go to whoever you choose on your leadership team
